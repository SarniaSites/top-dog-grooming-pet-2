Laura and Stuart

Laura has walked and groomed other people’s dogs since she was a girl. In 2016 she and her husband Stuart left their careers in finance and construction to make it their job.

They began with an Open Day, and Top Dog opened its doors on 29 October 2016. It has grown mostly by word of mouth, and it is still a small family business.

Plenty of today’s regulars first came in as puppies for a first groom, and now come for day care and holidays too.

Questions owners ask

Do you groom all breeds?

Yes. The salon takes every breed, from small terriers and Yorkies to spaniels, doodles and retrievers, and puppies coming in for their first groom.

Is boarding in kennels?

No. Boarding dogs stay in Laura and Stuart’s home and have the run of the house, apart from the bedrooms.

Will I hear how my dog is getting on?

Yes. While your dog is boarding we send photos and messages, so you can see the walks and the play.

How do I book?

Grooms can be booked online at any time. For day care, evening care and boarding, call or text 07781 130948, email, or use the form below.

Do you sell dog food?

We stock GURU dog and puppy food and can deliver it to your door. Ask Laura which one suits your dog.
