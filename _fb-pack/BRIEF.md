🎉🐾 TOP DOG IS 10 TODAY! 🐾🎉

10 years of happy paws, wagging tails and wonderful memories! 💚

Today marks a very special milestone for Top Dog as we celebrate our 10th anniversary!

But the story really began long before we opened our doors on 29 October 2016...

Laura's love of dogs started long before Top Dog. As a child, she would happily walk and groom her neighbours' dogs for free, simply because she loved spending time with them. She even organised a big dog agility show featuring some of the neighbourhood dogs! Looking back, perhaps the signs of her future career were there all along. 🐾 🐶

Fast forward a few years, and Laura's childhood love of dogs had grown into a dream of working with them every day. It was a dream her husband Stuart came to share, and together we decided to take a leap of faith, leaving behind our careers in finance (Laura) and construction (Stuart) to turn that dream into reality.

We started with an Open Day, and from there, thanks largely to word of mouth and the wonderful support of our customers, Top Dog has grown into the business it is today.

Over the past decade, we've had the pleasure of welcoming so many wonderful dogs and their owners into our lives. From playful puppies visiting us for the very first time to familiar furry faces who have been part of our Top Dog family for years, it's been an incredible journey.

For us, it's always been about more than grooming, daycare and boarding. It's about building relationships, earning your trust and making sure every dog in our care feels loved, happy and at home.
